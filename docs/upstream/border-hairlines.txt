Summary
-------
Thin borders vanish at screen zoom in poppler-based viewers: each border side is painted through a clip, half
the border's width for a collapsed table border

Description
-----------
BorderPainter.drawBorderSegment paints each side of a border rectangle as a line of the border's width inside a
clip: a trapezoid that gives the side its share of the two corners, and, for a collapsed border between two
cells (BorderProps.Mode.COLLAPSE_INNER), only the half of the border on the cell's own side, the neighbouring
cell painting the other half. So a 0.48pt rule between two cells is painted twice, each time through a clip
0.24pt wide.

Poppler (Okular, Evince, pdftoppm) widens a thin line, and a filled path that is a single rectangle, to at least
one whole device pixel, snapping its edges to the pixel grid. It does not do that to a clip. At screen zoom the
clip is a fraction of a pixel: where the snapped line falls in a pixel the clip barely reaches, nothing is
painted. The table rules come and go as the user zooms. A word processor's PDF of the same table, which fills a
rectangle per border, keeps them at every zoom.

FO (attached; base-14 fonts, no configuration): a two-row table, border-collapse="collapse", every cell
border="0.48pt solid black". Rendered on main with pdftoppm -gray -r N, the darkness of the five vertical
borders in the first row (0 invisible, 255 black):

{noformat}
 dpi   main
  60    14  23  15 197   0
  72    16  29  16  14   0
  80    15 197  90 111   0
  96   166 197 197  90 255
 110   166 197  18 177   0
 144    19 166 166 177 166
{noformat}

(A reading near 15 is the cell's text inside the measuring window: the border is gone.) The horizontal rules are
thinned too, and lost at some zooms. The table's outer edge, whose clip spans the whole border width, is lost as
well, so the full-width clip of the separate border model is not safe either. table-border-overpaint (FOP-2536)
does not help: its merged segments are painted through the same half-width clip; with it on, the rules still
vanished at 60, 72, 80 and 110 dpi.

The clip matters only where the borders meeting at a corner differ: in colour, in style (dashes, double), with a
radius, or with a translucent colour that would darken where painted twice. Where a side's neighbours are solid
in its colour, or absent, the corners take the same paint whether each side paints its mitred share or the whole
corner, and the two cells of a collapsed border resolve to the same border and paint the same line.

Fix (pull request to follow)
----------------------------
In BorderPainter.drawBorderSegment: a side that is solid, opaque and square-cornered, whose neighbouring sides
are absent or solid and opaque in the same colour, is painted as the same line over the same extent without the
clip. Every other side keeps the clip.

Measured on main with the change, the FO above: every vertical border 255 at 60, 72, 80, 90, 96, 100, 110, 120
and 144 dpi; the page's clip operators go from 32 to 0. Painted without anti-aliasing at 1200 dpi, the PDF is the
same but for one pixel at each of the two outer top corners (0.06pt); the PostScript output, painted by
Ghostscript at 1200 dpi, is pixel for pixel the same. PNG and TIFF output do not go through this painter
(Java2DPainter's drawBorderRect has five parameters, so AbstractIFPainter's six-parameter method fills
rectangles), and AFP has its own. Only painting changes, never layout. BorderPainterTestCase gains two tests: solid
borders of one colour in the three border modes paint four lines and no clip, over the whole side and width; a
red side among black ones clips itself and the two sides it meets, and dashed, translucent and rounded borders
clip every side.
