Summary
-------
simulate-style slants an italic face again and strokes a bold face again, and its stroke width does not scale
with the font size

Description
-----------
A font entry with simulate-style="true" makes FOP synthesise the style a triplet asks for from the face the entry
registers: PDFPainter.startSimulateStyle strokes a triplet of weight 700 (2 Tr) with a 0.31543pt line, and shears
a triplet of style italic by 0.3333 in the text matrix. It decides from the entry's flag and the triplet alone,
never from the face, although FOP has the face's italic angle (post table) and weight class (OS/2) in CustomFont.
So:

- an italic face registered for a bold italic triplet, as a family with an italic face and no bold one needs, is
  slanted a second time on top of its own slant;
- a bold face registered for a bold italic triplet, as a family with a bold face and no bold italic one needs, is
  stroked a second time;
- the stroke is a fixed 0.31543pt whatever the size, which is about right at 11pt only: at 22pt it is half the
  weight it should be, at 8pt about 40% too heavy.

Configuration and FO (Carlito, 24pt):

{noformat}
<font simulate-style="true" embed-url="file:///usr/share/fonts/carlito/Carlito-Italic.ttf"><font-triplet name="ItalFace" style="italic" weight="bold"/></font>
<font simulate-style="true" embed-url="file:///usr/share/fonts/carlito/Carlito-Regular.ttf"><font-triplet name="RegFace" style="italic" weight="bold"/></font>
<font simulate-style="true" embed-url="file:///usr/share/fonts/carlito/Carlito-Bold.ttf"><font-triplet name="BoldFace" style="italic" weight="bold"/></font>

<fo:flow flow-name="xsl-region-body" font-size="24pt" font-style="italic" font-weight="bold">
  <fo:block font-family="ItalFace">Italic face</fo:block>
  <fo:block font-family="RegFace">Regular face</fo:block>
  <fo:block font-family="BoldFace">Bold face</fo:block>
</fo:flow>
{noformat}

On main, the content stream draws all three the same way: 2 Tr 0.31543 w, and 1 0 0.3333 -1 ... Tm. The italic
face is slanted twice and the bold face stroked twice.

Fix (pull request to follow)
----------------------------
In PDFPainter.startSimulateStyle and endSimulateStyle: shear an italic triplet only where the face's italic angle
is 0; stroke a bold triplet only where the face's weight class is below 700 (an unknown weight, 0, counts as not
bold, as now); and stroke at the font size / 35. A stroke of 1/35 em is what Microsoft Word draws for a synthetic
bold, measured at 7.92 to 48pt in Word's own PDFs, and at 11.04pt it is the present 0.31543. Word also never
shears a face that is already italic, and its synthetic italic is the same 0.3333 shear FOP uses.

Measured on main with the change, the FO above: the italic face 2 Tr 0.68571 w and 1 0 0 -1 ... Tm (stroked,
not slanted again); the regular face stroked and sheared; the bold face sheared, not stroked. Only the painted
glyphs change, never layout. PDFPainterTestCase gains three cases (the italic face, the bold face, the stroke at
22.08pt), each failing before. The fop-core suite passes (3679 tests) and checkstyle is clean.
