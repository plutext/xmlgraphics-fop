# docx4j-fo-renderer 2.11-docx4j.5 (DRAFT)

Draft; closes when Jason says the release goes. Follows 2.11-docx4j.4 (2026-10-04). The runbook is
`docs/developer/releasing.md`.

`docx4j-fo-renderer` is a modified distribution derived from Apache FOP, maintained for docx4j's
docx-to-PDF path. Apache FOP is a trademark of the Apache Software Foundation; this is not an Apache
release. Packages stay `org.apache.fop`; the fork and Apache FOP must never share a classpath.
Coordinates `org.docx4j:docx4j-fo-renderer`, `-core`, `-events`, `-util`.

## Fixes since 2.11-docx4j.4

- **A page label of "0" is written to the PDF** (`fop/CR-012`, Enterprise CR-001 item 38). The page
  labels counted a decimal label's leading zeros past the end of "0" and threw; a page numbered 0 is now a
  `/P` prefix label.

- **A font's descender is not above its baseline, nor 0 for want of glyphs to guess from** (`fop/CR-015`,
  Enterprise CR-001 item 40, FOP-1896; capability `ascender-descender`). Two defects in how FOP reads a TrueType
  or OpenType font's ascender and descender:
  - It took an OS/2 typo descender above the baseline, a sign error in Wingdings 1 to 3 and the Lucida faces.
    A line in Wingdings came out about half Word's height, and an underline in Lucida Sans ran through the
    letters.
  - It gave a font without `d` and `p` glyphs, whose ascender and descender exceed the em, an ascender and
    descender of 0, so the text sat in the middle of its line. That is most Noto fonts for scripts other than
    Latin, MT Extra and Algerian: 978 of the 2,547 fonts measured.

  Wingdings now has Word's metrics: a 12.19pt text area at 11pt, baseline 9.88pt down, against Word's 12.21pt
  and 9.89pt. Gated through docx4j: three documents moved, all in an affected face (Noto Sans Devanagari +9
  lines, Noto Sans Ethiopic +2, and that document's other copy −1), none outside; 5975 holds 1.0000.

## Hooks since 2.11-docx4j.4

- **`continuation-display-align`** (`fop/CR-013`): `fox:continuation-display-align` on `fo:table-cell` sets
  the display-align of a cell's parts after the first when it breaks across pages, as Word sets a broken
  cell's later part from the top whatever its vertical alignment. The default, `auto`, keeps
  `display-align`. Gated through docx4j: without the attribute nothing moves; with it ten documents move up,
  none down, and the row-split probe reaches 1.0000 in all four compatibility modes.

- **`to-unicode-map`** (`fop/CR-014`): `<to-unicode code-point="F04A" unicode="263A"/>` children of a `<font>`
  configuration entry set the text the ToUnicode CMap publishes for a glyph reached through that code point.
  A symbol font's private-use text (Wingdings, Symbol, Webdings) then extracts, searches and reads aloud as its
  Unicode equivalent. The text layer only; glyphs and layout are unchanged. Gated through docx4j: nothing
  moves with or without the entries, and corpus document 5975's smileys read U+263A and U+1F610 where they
  read U+F04A and U+F04B.

- **`page-number-zero`** (`fop/CR-012`): `FOUserAgent.setPageNumberZeroAllowed(true)` keeps
  `initial-page-number="0"` as 0 (Word's cover page, `w:pgNumType w:start="0"`), where FOP's default, the
  XSL error recovery, makes it 1. Off by default. Gated through docx4j: without the call nothing moves;
  with it, four corpus documents that number a cover page 0 move up and nothing else does.
