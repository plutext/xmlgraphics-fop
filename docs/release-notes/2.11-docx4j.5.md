# docx4j-fo-renderer 2.11-docx4j.5 (DRAFT)

Draft; closes when Jason says the release goes. Follows 2.11-docx4j.4 (2026-10-04). The runbook is
`docs/developer/releasing.md`.

`docx4j-fo-renderer` is a modified distribution derived from Apache FOP, maintained for docx4j's
docx-to-PDF path. Apache FOP is a trademark of the Apache Software Foundation; this is not an Apache
release. Packages stay `org.apache.fop`; the fork and Apache FOP must never share a classpath.
Coordinates `org.docx4j:docx4j-fo-renderer`, `-core`, `-events`, `-util`.

## Fixes since 2.11-docx4j.4

- **A page label of "0" is written to the PDF** (`fop/CR-012`, Enterprise CR-001 item 38). The page
  labels counted a decimal label's leading zeros past the end of "0" and threw; a page numbered 0 is now a
  `/P` prefix label.

- **A font's descender is not above its baseline, nor 0 for want of glyphs to guess from** (`fop/CR-015`,
  Enterprise CR-001 item 40, FOP-1896; capability `ascender-descender`). Two defects in how FOP reads a TrueType
  or OpenType font's ascender and descender:
  - It took an OS/2 typo descender above the baseline, a sign error in Wingdings 1 to 3 and the Lucida faces.
    A line in Wingdings came out about half Word's height, and an underline in Lucida Sans ran through the
    letters.
  - It gave a font without `d` and `p` glyphs, whose ascender and descender exceed the em, an ascender and
    descender of 0, so the text sat in the middle of its line. That is most Noto fonts for scripts other than
    Latin, MT Extra and Algerian: 978 of the 2,547 fonts measured.

  Wingdings now has Word's metrics: a 12.19pt text area at 11pt, baseline 9.88pt down, against Word's 12.21pt
  and 9.89pt. Gated through docx4j: three documents moved, all in an affected face (Noto Sans Devanagari +9
  lines, Noto Sans Ethiopic +2, and that document's other copy −1), none outside; 5975 holds 1.0000.

- **A letter a font splits into its base and an accent reads correctly in the text layer** (`fop/CR-016`,
  Enterprise CR-001 item 42). Cambria Regular's substitutions draw à as `a` plus a combining grave, ά as alpha
  plus a tonos. Since 2.11-docx4j.2 (`fop/CR-002`) the base glyph was published as the accented letter. Every
  plain a, e, o, u, c, α or A in the same font then copied out of the PDF accented, and the accented letters
  doubled their accent. Each glyph now publishes its own piece, so the text reads as written: in decomposed
  form, the same text once normalised. This also recovers accents stock FOP lost to private-use code points.
  Nothing is drawn differently. Gated through docx4j: nine documents up, none down, page images identical;
  corpus document 8371 from 0.16 to 0.99.

- **A side float ends where the text below it starts, and never inside a table** (`fop/CR-020`, Enterprise
  CR-001 items 44 and 45; capability `side-float-edges`). Two defects in FOP's side floats:
  - A float ended only where a line box reached its foot, so the space between two paragraphs was not counted:
    after a space that carried the next line below the float, that line was still narrowed beside it. The space
    at the float's edge was also discarded, as at a page break. The float now ends at the break before the
    first line lying wholly below its foot, and the space there is kept.
  - A float whose end fell inside a table threw `NoSuchElementException`, since a table's layout cannot be
    restarted from a row. The float now ends at the first break after the table.

  Gated through docx4j: one document moved, corpus document 5075 from 0.7660 to 0.9362, its first two pages
  now holding Word's lines; nothing else. With docx4j's text-box band switched on, its probes match Word line
  for line.

## Hooks since 2.11-docx4j.4

- **`continuation-display-align`** (`fop/CR-013`): `fox:continuation-display-align` on `fo:table-cell` sets
  the display-align of a cell's parts after the first when it breaks across pages, as Word sets a broken
  cell's later part from the top whatever its vertical alignment. The default, `auto`, keeps
  `display-align`. Gated through docx4j: without the attribute nothing moves; with it ten documents move up,
  none down, and the row-split probe reaches 1.0000 in all four compatibility modes.

- **`page-master-by-content`** (`fop/CR-017`): `fox:page-sequence-master-reference` on a block-level FO in the main
  flow names a page-sequence-master. The pages whose first line lies at or after that FO take their masters from
  it, as a word processor takes a page's margins, header and footer from the section owning its first line. So a
  run of continuous sections merged into one page-sequence keeps each section's vertical margins. A section
  starting part-way down a page changes nothing until the next page, and uses its first-page master only on a page
  it opens. Inert without the attribute. Gated through docx4j (CR-031 phase 2): without the attribute nothing
  moves; with it, 12802's pages 3 to 6 come within 2pt of Word's, 7235 gains 22 lines, 5507 reaches Word's page
  count, and the continuous-section probe's page parity rises from 0.917 to 0.959.

- **`page-number-restart`** (`fop/CR-017.2`): `fox:page-number-restart="S"` on a marked part restarts its page
  numbers as Word restarts a continuous section's. The page the part starts on prints its owner's number, and the
  count begins there. With `fox:page-number-restart-parity="keep"` (odd and even headers), it begins on the next
  page where S's parity differs. Headers, `fo:page-number`, citations and PDF page labels follow the count, and a
  following `auto` page-sequence continues from it. Gated through docx4j: the restart probes reach Word's folios
  (line parity 1.0000), and nothing else moves but one document, whose table of contents now prints Word's number.
  Also: a marked part starting part-way down a page-sequence's first page after a span change no longer
  overflows that page (`fop/CR-017` §12).

- **`measured-region-extents`** (`fop/CR-018`): `fox:extent="measured"` on `fo:region-before` or
  `fo:region-after` makes the region as tall as its static content, measured per page-sequence and master. The
  body's margin on that side becomes the larger of its stated margin and that height, as Word places the body
  below a header and above a footer. So docx4j no longer needs the extra FOP render it ran to measure them.
  Inert without the attribute; the `lr-tb` and `rl-tb` writing modes at reference-orientation 0. Gated through
  docx4j: without the attribute nothing moves. With it, against docx4j's own measurement, nothing moves across
  the four corpora and 215 probes; 2 of 1,902 masters differ by more than 0.5pt, both in one document, where
  FOP's measurement is nearer Word's. Rendering is 7.6% faster. Where region-before or region-after has
  `precedence="true"`, region-start and region-end move with the measured extents (`fop/CR-018` §10).

- **`to-unicode-map`** (`fop/CR-014`): `<to-unicode code-point="F04A" unicode="263A"/>` children of a `<font>`
  configuration entry set the text the ToUnicode CMap publishes for a glyph reached through that code point.
  A symbol font's private-use text (Wingdings, Symbol, Webdings) then extracts, searches and reads aloud as its
  Unicode equivalent. The text layer only; glyphs and layout are unchanged. Gated through docx4j: nothing
  moves with or without the entries, and corpus document 5975's smileys read U+263A and U+1F610 where they
  read U+F04A and U+F04B.

- **`page-number-zero`** (`fop/CR-012`): `FOUserAgent.setPageNumberZeroAllowed(true)` keeps
  `initial-page-number="0"` as 0 (Word's cover page, `w:pgNumType w:start="0"`), where FOP's default, the
  XSL error recovery, makes it 1. Off by default. Gated through docx4j: without the call nothing moves;
  with it, four corpus documents that number a cover page 0 move up and nothing else does.

**Warnings of these hooks are FOP events.** A `fox:page-sequence-master-reference`, `fox:page-number-restart`,
`fox:page-number-restart-parity` or `fox:extent` that cannot be honoured is reported by a `WARN` event of
`BlockLevelEventProducer` (`pageMasterReference...`, `pageNumberRestart...`, `measuredExtentUnsupported`), naming
the FO it concerns, and ignored. An embedder's `EventListener` receives them. Without one, FOP's default logs them
under the `org.apache.fop.apps.FOUserAgent` logger, as it logs its own events, rather than the layout classes'
loggers (`fop/CR-017` §13, `fop/CR-018` §10).
