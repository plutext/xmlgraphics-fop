# docx4j-fo-renderer 2.11-docx4j.6 (DRAFT)

Draft; closes when Jason says the release goes. Follows 2.11-docx4j.5 (2026-10-07). The runbook is
`docs/developer/releasing.md`.

`docx4j-fo-renderer` is a modified distribution derived from Apache FOP, maintained for docx4j's
docx-to-PDF path. Apache FOP is a trademark of the Apache Software Foundation; this is not an Apache
release. Packages stay `org.apache.fop`; the fork and Apache FOP must never share a classpath.
Coordinates `org.docx4j:docx4j-fo-renderer`, `-core`, `-events`, `-util`.

## Changes since 2.11-docx4j.5

- **`clear` is honoured on a block-level FO after a side float** (`fop/CR-022`; capability
  `clear-after-side-float`). An `fo:block`, `fo:block-container`, `fo:table` or `fo:list-block` whose `clear` names
  the side a float is on ends the float at the break before it and is laid out at the float's foot, full width, as
  CSS clearance places a box below the floats it must not sit beside. FOP read `clear` on `fo:float` only, so a table
  following a text box's anchor paragraph was set beside the box and drawn over it. Content without `clear`, or with
  `clear` on the other side, is set beside the float as before. Gated through docx4j: with nothing written, nothing
  moves on the four corpora and the 260 probes; with `clear` on a table that follows a text box's band, corpus document
  10855's rubric table goes below the box at Word's position, 1.4pt off, where it had been drawn over the box, and the
  document reaches Word's page count; the text-box-beside probes reach 1.0000.

- **A side float's edge is taken where it falls, and never thrown over** (`fop/CR-022` §3.5, found by the docx4j
  session's gate on corpus document 4083). The edge search had passed over an edge on a page whose ratio was within
  range, had never offered a break under a keep as the edge, and threw `NullPointerException` in `handleFloat` when a
  deferred edge met a forced break inside a table. Now the algorithm ends at the edge whatever the page's ratio,
  keeps do not hide it (the edge is no page break), a table the page cannot hold starts at the float's foot rather
  than having the edge deferred into it, and a float that still cannot end is given up with a warning rather than an
  exception. Apache `main` fails earlier on the same document (FOP-3354). Gated through docx4j: nothing moves on the
  floats docx4j emits; 4083 renders where it had thrown.

## Hooks since 2.11-docx4j.5

- **`float-offset`** (`fop/CR-023` §4.1): `fox:float-offset` on `fo:float` draws the float that far below the top of
  its anchor block, the block's own space-before included and the previous block's space-after not, as a word
  processor places a table a stated distance below the paragraph
  it is anchored to; the lines above the float keep the full width, the first line the float's top falls in and
  those down to its foot are set beside it; a float the page cannot hold below its anchor goes to the next page with
  its anchor, and several floats on one anchor are drawn each at its own offset. Without the attribute nothing
  changes. Gated through docx4j, which writes it for Word's `tblpY` on floating tables, text-box bands, anchored
  pictures and frames: the floating-table probes rise (0.69 to 0.98, 0.50 to 0.69, 0.78 to 0.97), two corpus documents
  reach Word's page count with 16 and 23 lines back, and nothing moves with the attribute not written.

- **`float-overflow-below`** (`fop/CR-023` §4.2): a line whose content does not fit beside a side float, but would
  fit the column without it, is set at the float's foot with the rest of its paragraph, full width, as a word
  processor moves a word that does not fit beside a table below it; FOP set the line beside the float and let it
  overflow the column. A word wider than the column stays where it was. Gated through docx4j: inert on its floating
  tables, where Word's in-flow table precedes the paragraphs; docx4j uses it for picture floats.

- **A table row at a page's foot needs room for its bottom margin** (`fop/CR-025`; hook `row-first-part-room`,
  `FOUserAgent.setRowFirstPartRoom(true)`, off by default).
  A row whose first line would fit only without the cell's bottom padding and border goes whole to the next page, as
  a word processor lays it; FOP started the row and split it after the line, the margin given up at the split. A row
  whose first line fits with the margin is started and may split after it, as before. Gated through docx4j (b208):
  11657's row at page 30's foot moved whole as Word's, 2451 +19 lines, nothing else moved.

- **Columns of unequal width in the region body** (`fop/CR-026` phase A; capability `column-widths`,
  `fox:column-widths` and `fox:column-gaps` on `fo:region-body`). The body's columns take a width each and a gap per
  pair, as a word processor's section whose columns are not equal; FOP laid every column at one width from
  `column-count` and `column-gap`, so docx4j rendered such a section as a one-row table that cannot flow across pages,
  or at equal widths when longer than a page. Each column is laid at its width through FOP's own changing-IPD restart,
  the renderers step by each column's width and gap, and the area tree carries the lists. Lists that disagree with
  `column-count` or the body's width are ignored with a warning and the columns stay equal; a table that crosses a
  column boundary keeps the previous column's width for its remainder (FOP's non-restartable layout managers, as
  across pages); balancing before a `span="all"` block is not yet unequal-aware (phase B), the columns before it
  laid as they are. Gated through docx4j (b211): corpus document 10598 +203 lines (parity 0.21 to 0.75, 10 pages to 9
  against Word's 8, its right column at Word's x), 6116 +14 at Word's 5 pages, nothing else moved; docx4j writes the
  widths where the stretch does not fit a page and the columns differ by more than 5%, and keeps its one-row table
  otherwise.
